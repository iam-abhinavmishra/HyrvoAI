HyrvoAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

HyrvoAI is an AI-powered internal knowledge and helpdesk platform designed to help organizations access information from their internal policies, manuals, and other authorized documents.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how HyrvoAI is configured and used, the platform may collect and process the following information:

  • Name and email address associated with an account.
  • Authentication information required to access the platform.
  • OAuth account information when Google or LinkedIn sign-in is used.
  • Questions and messages submitted through the HyrvoAI assistant.
  • Information contained in documents uploaded by authorized administrators.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by HyrvoAI is used to:

  • Authenticate users and provide access to their accounts.
  • Provide the HyrvoAI AI assistant.
  • Retrieve relevant information from authorized company documents.
  • Maintain chat sessions and conversation history where enabled.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the platform.

3. Google and LinkedIn Sign-In

HyrvoAI may allow users to sign in using Google or LinkedIn. When a user chooses a third-party sign-in provider, HyrvoAI may receive information made available by that provider, such as the user's name, email address, and provider account identifier.

HyrvoAI uses this information to authenticate the user and associate the authenticated account with the appropriate HyrvoAI account.

4. Company Documents

HyrvoAI may process documents uploaded by authorized administrators of an organization. These documents may contain internal company information and are intended to be accessible only to authorized users according to the organization's access-control configuration.

5. AI Processing

HyrvoAI processes user questions and relevant document content to generate responses. The system is designed to use authorized company information when answering questions.

6. Data Security

HyrvoAI uses authentication, authorization, access controls, and other technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, preserve authorized company records, or satisfy applicable operational and security requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

HyrvoAI may use third-party services for authentication and other infrastructure functions. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. User Rights and Requests

Users may contact their organization's HyrvoAI administrator regarding account information, access, correction, or deletion requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the HyrvoAI platform, functionality, or data-processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning HyrvoAI, please contact the administrator or organization responsible for your HyrvoAI deployment.
